# WFC 1.2: Release review

Demo for the board. The board moves this card to *Done* when approved, after which the release is tagged `wfc_generator-v1.2.0`.

## Review checklist for the board
1. Open `hillside_ground` and generate with several seeds: the rock floor comes from the boundary socket, and tower tops carry lights.
2. Give a tile a height curve and check where it appears.
3. Drag the bounds and pin box handles, pin part of a result, and regenerate.
4. Regenerate and check that markers are replaced, and that undo brings them back.
5. Read the acceptance results.
