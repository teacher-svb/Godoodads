# godoowave 1.7: 2D: mirrored variants

`TileDefinition` gets a Mirror. The variant compiler adds mirrored variants next to the rotations, swapping `+`/`-` on every one-sided socket, and merges duplicates as today. Each variant knows its rotation and mirror for the writer.
