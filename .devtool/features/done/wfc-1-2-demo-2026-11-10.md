# WFC 1.2: Demo with boundary sockets and markers

`BuildWfcDemo`: `hillside_ground` uses a `rock` bottom boundary socket instead of a painted rock layer. Add markers, for example a light on tower tops.

Brief: §12.6.

## Done when
- 20-seed reliability at 100×16×100 for both scenes, compared with 1.1 (1.65 s and 2.34 s, 20 of 20).
- A windowed render compared with `docs/hillside.png`.

## Result
- `hillside_ground` has no painted cells. Its settings set the bottom boundary socket to `rock`. Tower Top has a marker, `assets/tower_light.tscn`: a warm OmniLight3D (range 7) with a small emissive flame, built by `BuildWfcDemo`.
- Reliability at 100×16×100, 20 seeds, 0 backtracks and 0 restarts:

| Scene | 1.1 | 1.2 |
|---|---|---|
| Ground (1.1: painted rock layer; 1.2: rock boundary) | 20 of 20, 1.65 s | 20 of 20, 0.69 s |
| No fixed cells | 20 of 20, 2.34 s | 20 of 20, 0.81 s (identical results) |

- With the boundary, every tile count equals 1.1 except rock, which drops by exactly the 10,000 painted cells. The terrain is the 1.1 terrain moved down one layer.
- Windowed renders at 32×12×32 (seed 2, by day and by night) look like `docs/hillside.png`. Seven tower tops carry lights. The image is kept.
- Engine tests updated: the demo scenes have no painted cells and the ground scene has the rock boundary. A new test solves on the rock boundary and plans one tower light per Tower Top.
