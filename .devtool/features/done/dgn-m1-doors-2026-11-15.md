# DGN M1: Door placement

Doors on the sides that face each graph neighbour, never on corners, never next to another door on the same side, never facing the bounds edge or an obstacle without a free cell in between. Max Doors Per Room drops edges while the graph stays connected.

Brief: §4.3.

## Done when
- Unit tests over 200 seeds: every rule holds, every room has a door, and the door graph is connected.

## Results
Branch `godoodungeon-m1`.

- `Core/DoorPlacer.cs`: `Place(layout, settings, occupied)` fills `layout.Doors` from the rooms and edges (tree edges first, then the loops, shortest first).
  - For each edge, both rooms get a door on the side facing the other room (the side most aligned with the direction between the centres; X wins ties). The door goes at the non-corner wall cell closest to the other room's centre.
  - **One door per side.** An edge that leaves through a side that already has a door reuses it. So "never next to another door on the same side" always holds.
  - **The lane rule.** Outside the door, max(Wall Clearance, 1) cells plus one more must be inside the bounds, free of occupied cells and outside every other room grown by the clearance. Otherwise the next cell along the side is tried, then the next facing side.
  - **Max Doors Per Room** caps new doors. An edge that needs one more uses the room's door that faces closest to the other room, so no edge is dropped. A room without any door tries every side. If none works, the room gets a layout error ("Room n … has no wall where a corridor can leave it").
- `DungeonLayout.Errors` and `IsValid`: errors make the layout unusable; warnings do not.
- `LayoutSettings.MinimumGap` = max(2 × clearance + 1, **2**). With clearance 0, a gap of 1 would let a door's corridor piece face the neighbouring room's wall, which the corridor solve excludes, so guided paths would count it as connected. Brief §4.3 is updated, and so is the door rule (one door per side, the cap reuses a door instead of dropping edges).

Checks: Core tests **292 passed** (8 new in `DungeonDoorTests`, 1 added in `DungeonLayoutModelTests`):
- Over 200 seeds with the defaults: every room has 1 to Max doors, at most one per side, on a wall and never a corner, with a free lane plus one cell inside the bounds and clear of other rooms. No layout errors.
- The same rules over 5 variants × 50 seeds, on 64×64 with a hand-placed wall: clearance 2 with gap 5; clearance 0 with gap 2 and every edge; Max Doors 1; Max Doors 2 with half the loops; 30 small rooms.
- Max Doors 1 gives exactly one door per room. Two rooms get doors facing each other at the expected cells. Three rooms where two edges leave the same side share one door. A room hemmed in by hand-placed cells and the bounds edge gives the layout error. Doors are fixed for a seed.

## For the M1 review
The defaults give many doors (seed 1: 10 rooms, 14 edges of which 5 are loops, 24 doors). Every edge tends to use its own side. Lowering Extra Connections or Max Doors Per Room thins them out. Worth judging in the ASCII layouts.
